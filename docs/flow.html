The connections between the tracks

  1. 1
    A → B3 · catalog and photos. Photo selection pulls from track A the already curated bank (A4) and the usage log.
  2. 2
    A1 → B4 · design system components. The review board assembly uses the Social/* components.
  3. 3
    B7 → A4 · uses, history and timings. Each delivery logs the photos used, and the bank knows what has already gone out.
  4. 4
    B6.1 → A1 · learned criteria. What was corrected in review becomes a written rule, and the next review board is born with it.

Principles that run through everything

  • A rule is not a filter: only the non-negotiable blocks; the rest becomes an exception with a reason for a human decision.
  • Nothing goes out without a person: publishing, delivering and changing Notion only with confirmation.
  • Time is measured: every review board logs its milestones in projects/<slug>/production/timings.csv.
  • Feedback becomes learning: it goes to projects/<slug>/learnings.md or docs/carousel-learnings.md.
